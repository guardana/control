Approvals and pauses

Held calls

Anyone who can write the approvals directory can answer these. The fields shown come from a projection and are not bound to the action digest beside them: the approval binds the exact arguments, which no record holds.

An answer takes two clicks: the second confirms the call by its tool, its upstream and the start of its action digest. Cards keep their place; a new held call is added after them.

Pauses

This page cannot tell whether a plane reads this pause file. A pause blocks new calls it covers; it does not stop a call already running.
