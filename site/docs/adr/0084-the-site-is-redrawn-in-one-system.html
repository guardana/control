ADR-0084: The site is redrawn: one system for the landing page and the docs, a light and a dark theme, llms.txt

Status: accepted Date: 2026-11-13

Amends ADR-0030 on the fonts and their licence, the visual system's path, the landing page's diagrams and what its checks hold; ADR-0031 on the pages' look and the files the renderer writes; ADR-0009 on which third-party fonts the site ships under the SIL Open Font License; and ADR-0024 on the visual system, which is no longer a copy of Guardana's brand.

Context

The landing page described the 1.0 goal before what a reader can do today, listed status rows by their inventory names, and drew its figures as cards that a phone stacked into a long column. The documentation pages had a different look over the same tokens, with no dark theme a reader could choose. The maintainer approved a new page: a headline about deciding what agents run and checking what they did, a scene of one agent's three calls, the three steps, six questions a reader asks, the chain of what is signed, a concept of a run drawn as a graph, a quickstart, the limits, and the questions before connecting it, in a light and a dark theme on a phone and a desktop. The public line moves with it, so README, the page and the social card change together.

Some of the site's checks described the old page: a list of five to eight status items titled with their rows' names, a list of backlog items titled with their tasks, and at least three figures drawn as cards.

Decision

  • One visual system. The tokens, the fonts and their licence are site/assets/control/v2/, pinned by the digest of its SHA256SUMS as brand v1 was: a change is a v3 at a new path. The fonts are Geist and Geist Mono, one variable WOFF2 file each from the fonts' own v1.9.2 release, under the SIL Open Font License 1.1, its text served beside them. Nothing loads brand v1 any more, so site/assets/brand/v1/ and its IBM Plex files are removed rather than kept unread. control.css holds what every page wears: the base, the header, the buttons and the footer; home.css the landing page; docs.css the documentation pages, with the names the drawn diagrams read mapped onto the v2 tokens. Only tokens.css sets a token, and no stylesheet writes words through content.
  • A light and a dark theme, with no script. The tokens follow prefers-color-scheme. A checkbox in the shared header selects the other theme through html:has(#theme:checked); the choice lasts for the page, not across pages, because nothing stores it.
  • One header and one footer. Both are written by the renderer and carried byte for byte by the landing page, the header with the release pill in its slot.
  • The landing page draws its own illustrations. An animated scene of three calls in SVG with SMIL, a three-row list in its place below 1180 px, where the scene's labels would draw under 12 px, and the concept graph; each is an element with role="img" and a text alternative. Every animation starts near its end and every element rests in its final state, so a still frame shows the outcome. With reduced motion the CSS animations stop, the hero shows the list, and the graph shows its complete state. It draws no README block, and README holds no diagram the page needs.
  • Every label is tied to its source. The words implemented, experimental and planned appear on the page only as a st element inside an element whose data-status names a component row and shows that row's label, or as planned inside an element whose data-backlog names a task and shows its id, linked to the backlog. Each such element carries the row's name or the task's Work cell as its title, and a reviewed list in the test pairs each element's heading with its row or task, so a card cannot claim a row it does not describe. A sentence that calls something planned, or promises later work, sits inside such an element; in README it names its task.
  • llms.txt. The renderer writes site/llms.txt: the product's name, README's lede as the description, the status in one paragraph, then every page under its type's heading with its address and summary, and the records under "Optional". The sitemap keeps listing pages only. A concatenated llms-full.txt is not written: the pages and records together are over 1 MB of Markdown.
  • The public line. The headline is "Decide what your AI agents run. Check what they did.", the lede the sentence under it, word for word in README, on the page and in llms.txt; the category phrase, AI agent supervision framework, stays in the page's title, its description and README.

Security / compatibility impact

No product code changes. The page still runs no script and loads nothing from another host under the same _headers; the theme switch and the quickstart tabs are checkboxes and radio buttons that CSS reads. The removed brand v1 files were reachable at their addresses; nothing of this project links them.

Alternatives considered

  • Keeping brand v1 and adding Geist beside it. Two type systems in one tree, and files nobody loads kept as if they were in use.
  • A script that remembers the theme. It would break script-src 'none'.
  • The release pill in the hero as well as the header. Two pills on one page to keep equal; the header carries it on every page already.
  • Keeping the status list titled with the rows' names. The inventory's wording is precise and long; the page's headings ask a reader's questions, and the row stays named in data-status and title.
  • llms-full.txt for the pages alone. About as large as the 1 MB the file is meant to stay under, and a partial copy would read as the whole.

Consequences

A row whose label changes, a task that leaves the backlog, or a card whose heading changes fails the site check until the page or the reviewed list follows. A theme a reader picks is forgotten on the next page.

Validation

Tests in internal/docscheck/ and internal/docscheck/sitedoc/docsite/, each with fixtures that make it fail:

  • the v2 directory matches its SHA256SUMS, whose digest is pinned, and the site holds exactly the pinned manifest and what the renderer writes;
  • every data-status names a row, carries its name as title and shows its label once; every data-backlog names a task, carries its Work cell as title and shows its id once; each pairs with its heading as the reviewed list says, and the page ties at least three distinct tasks;
  • a label word in any text of the page outside those elements is refused, and so is a sentence that calls something planned or promises later work outside one, on the page and in README;
  • no page holds a script element or an event handler, and no attribute holds an address of another host other than a link, read from the parsed page; no SMIL element animates a reference;
  • every page holds one theme checkbox with its label and the tokens stylesheet, and the switched palettes equal the other scheme's; no other stylesheet sets a token or writes words through content;
  • every page holds one footer, and the landing page's header and footer are the bytes of every documentation page's;
  • the page's illustrations each carry a text alternative, at least three;
  • the page's lede is README's, and llms.txt quotes it, says alpha and not a security boundary, and lists every page under its type with its address and summary; a README with no lede stops the render.

Rendered from docs/adr/0084-the-site-is-redrawn-in-one-system.md on main. It describes the tree as it is now; a release's notes say what a tag holds. The latest release is v0.17.0, and its documentation is in its tag.
